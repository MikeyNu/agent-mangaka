## What was actually inspected

The supplied folder contained **52 WebP files representing 51 unique images**.
`008 (1).webp` and `008 (2).webp` have identical SHA-256 hashes. Every strip
received full-length tiled overview inspection, and **87 distinct selected
native-resolution regions** received detail inspection. There are 89 crop-to-file
bindings because two inspected regions apply to the duplicated source image.

The coverage record (in the separate study records) contains source dimensions,
hashes, exact crop rectangles, reviewer scope, visible observations, inferred
functions, transferable rules and excluded genre details. Overview inspection
is at reduced scale. This is not a claim that every pixel or every panel was
inspected at native size. Source filenames and suffixes are inventory labels;
they do not establish chapter numbers or narrative chronology.

The source images and their crops remain private. This public skill records
analytical findings without redistributing the source comic. The original art
in the live trial was generated separately for this evaluation.

## Structural and artistic observations

### 1. Geography appears before selective coverage

Elevated group views show people, floor patterns, furniture, paths and nearby
objects before closer reactions. `002 (4).webp`, crop `[0,550,760,1950]`, is a
clear room example. `010 (3).webp` also establishes a larger group and discarded
equipment. These images make later selective views easier to orient.

**Transfer:** plan all participants and landmarks from above, then select the
camera for each beat. Keep an offscreen person in world state. A cropped frame
is not evidence of an exit. Review background relationships from the chosen
camera instead of pasting the same backdrop behind a character.

### 2. A vertical reveal can be one spatial journey

The unsuffixed `002.webp` uses a long progression through sky, facade, entrance
and interior. It changes scale and atmosphere before focusing the narrative.
The observable sequence supports an interpretation of gradual reveal and
anticipation; the original artist's intention is not independently documented.

**Transfer:** plan a continuous reveal through a modern station, apartment
building, forest or spacecraft when the story benefits from it. Do not import
the fortress simply because it was the source example. Preview the reveal
through a phone-sized viewport, including across export joins.

### 3. Motion is distributed across pose, scale and framing

The study combines enlarged foreground limbs or weapons, oblique frame edges,
reaching silhouettes, converging marks, selective softness and sharp focal
faces. Wide actor-to-target views often prepare closer action. The body and
contact geometry carry meaning even when movement lines are sparse.

**Transfer:** plan support, weight, trajectory, depth and the selected instant.
Use perspective to emphasize a near hand or foot while preserving a plausible
limb connection. Add motion marks to reinforce a readable action, not conceal
an unreadable pose. A punch's intended target and actual intercepted target
must remain distinct.

### 4. Reaction and stillness shape impact

Close reactions, isolated eyes, hands and object contacts punctuate wider
views. A relatively quiet frame can precede an abrupt diagonal or close impact.
The contrast changes attention and perceived rhythm without requiring every
panel to be visually loud.

**Transfer:** assign each panel a narrative job. Select anticipation, contact,
reaction or aftermath according to what the reader needs. Do not mechanically
add speed lines, bursts or tilted frames to every dramatic moment.

### 5. Acting can contradict spoken reassurance

`006 (1).webp` juxtaposes outward reassurance with contradictory private
thought. Other scenes show composed smiles, nervousness, contempt or social
performance. A smile alone does not establish happiness.

**Transfer:** record inner emotion, outward display, intention and intensity.
The skill should reject an unmotivated cheerful face in an argument, while
allowing a tense smile that serves the script. Hands, shoulders, gaze and
distance should support the same acting decision.

### 6. Balloon meaning is a project grammar

The corpus includes ordinary ovals, dotted thought connectors, dark fuzzy
internal forms, sharp shouting outlines, wavering shapes and changes in fill
or typography. Balloons can extend into whitespace. Their tails often imply
direction without touching the mouth.

**Transfer:** encode semantic channel separately from outline shape. Preserve
the supplied story's specific grammar. Bind text to the correct speaker and
source. Use separate transparent balloon and text layers, and inspect the
natural reading path after assembly. The actual source layer stack cannot be
recovered from these flattened images.

### 7. Simplification is a controlled expressive departure

Imagined conversation and miniature figures simplify a character while keeping
recognizable hair, costume or gesture. The deformation carries a particular
thought, reaction or comic beat. It is not a new permanent character design.

**Transfer:** declare the scope and purpose of chibi or symbolic treatment,
retain identity anchors, and plan the return to normal proportions. A chibi
figure can be physically acting or purely imagined; those cases have different
state consequences.

## Important exceptions

| Visible case | Interpretation to preserve | Required safeguard |
|---|---|---|
| `007 (2).webp` repeats one fighter through action phases | Temporal samples can show successive motion | Distinguish samples from three physical people |
| `008 (5).webp` uses faint arm echoes during panic | Motion exaggeration is deliberate | Permit declared echoes, reject an unexplained solid extra arm |
| `007 (3).webp` uses a miniature explanatory replay | The action is being re-explained | Hold present state and avoid applying injury twice |
| `006 (4).webp` shows imagined guardians | The mental image adds meaning | Do not add those guardians to the physical room cast |
| `011 (1).webp` includes imagined miniature interaction | Parody or impersonation is scoped | Do not replace normal canon or speaker identity |
| `009 (3).webp` shows cleanup while distress remains | Recovery can be partial | Clear only the condition changed by the visible cause |
| Unsuffixed `008.webp` places the floor along screen-left | A sideways presentation or rolled camera | Do not rotate world gravity automatically |
| Unsuffixed `010.webp` shows a cyan weapon-related light | A temporary event can alter illumination | Record event cause and duration |
| Unsuffixed `011.webp` uses warm vow emphasis | Color can serve expressive emphasis | Do not invent a persistent physical lamp |
| Unsuffixed `012.webp` presents a book account later disputed | Illustrated narration can be unreliable | Preserve source and truth status |
| Unsuffixed `013.webp` includes an ancestor portrait | Depicted identity is not a living occupant | Bind the portrait's canon without adding a physical speaker |

These observations led to explicit representation modes, provenance, full
scene state, temporary-light domains and repair rules. They are more useful
than a simplistic rule such as “never repeat a character” or “never smile
during conflict.” The plan determines whether the visual departure is
intentional and readable.

## Transfer across genres and formats

Separate the source's content from its craft:

| Transferable craft | Setting-specific source detail |
|---|---|
| A long approach that reveals scale | Castle, palace or medieval gate |
| A top-down plan maintaining screen geography | Stone hall or heraldic room decoration |
| Anticipation, contact and reaction | Swordsmanship or magic-system mechanics |
| Foreground foreshortening | Particular fantasy weapon |
| Selective edge and value emphasis | Magical glow or supernatural aura |
| Social subtext and reaction inserts | Noble rank or family lore |
| Thought grammar and chibi emphasis | Exact character, dialogue or costume |

A modern kitchen disagreement should use its own architecture, props, clothes,
lighting and behavior. A monochrome RTL manga page needs its own value/tone
system, page-turn reveals and lettering conventions. The source is a colored
vertical comic, so it does not by itself validate print pagination, every genre,
or every reading direction. The independent planning tasks and original live
trial exercise some transfers; future projects require their own visual QA.

## What the images cannot establish

Do not claim measured Fibonacci construction, an exact lens number, a specific
brush, hidden masks, source layer order, a fully manual workflow, or absence of
3D reference from flattened WebP files. The final project can require a
hand-drawn 2D appearance and use theoretical spatial construction without
rendered 3D, but that is a production choice rather than a proven fact about
the original artist's tools.

Similarly, analytical observations do not guarantee generated execution.
The native image trial needed camera and shadow repairs. Preserve that evidence
and keep actual visual acceptance separate from a passing planning manifest.
